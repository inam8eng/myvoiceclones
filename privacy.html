Privacy

SHORT VERSION: THERE IS NOTHING TO COLLECT

The app

VoiceClone runs entirely on your computer. Your recordings, your voices, and every file it makes stay in its folder on your disk. The app has no account system and no server side — your voice is never uploaded because it has nowhere to go.

The app touches the internet in exactly two cases: the one-time AI engine download when you install, and a license check with Gumroad when you activate a purchase. Neither sends audio anywhere, ever.

This website

No cookies. No analytics scripts. No tracking pixels. No forms. The only third-party request this page makes is loading its two fonts from Google Fonts; if that bothers you, the site works fine with fonts blocked.

Buying happens on Gumroad, under Gumroad's privacy policy — they handle payment and give you the download.

The rule the product is built on

Clone your own voice, or a voice whose owner said yes. VoiceClone is built for that, and only that.
